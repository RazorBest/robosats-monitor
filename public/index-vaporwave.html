[01] WHY ROBOSATS?

Centralized exchanges steal your privacy with mandatory KYC and passport scans. RoboSats operates over Tor & Lightning with disposable robot avatars and escrow deposits: 100% peer-to-peer, 0% KYC.

[02] WHY THIS MONITOR?

P2P orderbooks live across decentralized Nostr (NIP-69) relays. This terminal aggregates global order flow and computes 12-hour rolling premium curves so you never overpay for non-KYC bitcoin.

[03] WHAT IS A PREMIUM?

The price difference over spot market index. +3.00% = 3% markup for privacy. -1.50% = 1.5% discount below global market spot.

[04] ESCROW BONDS & SAFETY

Both trading peers lock a small refundable Lightning fidelity bond in multisig escrow. Upon fiat payment confirmation, bitcoin & bonds are released instantaneously.
