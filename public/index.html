Why RoboSats?

Centralized exchanges require KYC identity verification, passport scans, and custody of user funds. RoboSats enables private peer-to-peer (P2P) Bitcoin trading over Tor and the Lightning Network using disposable robot identities and refundable escrow bonds: no KYC, no accounts, and no personal data stored.

Why This Monitor?

P2P orderbooks are decentralized across Nostr relays (NIP-69) and Tor onions. This dashboard tracks real-time order depth and calculates rolling premium trends across currencies and payment methods so you know fair market rates before buying or selling.

What is a Premium?

The price difference compared to Bitcoin's spot market price. +3.00% means paying 3% above spot to acquire private, non-KYC Bitcoin. -1.50% means buying at a 1.5% discount below spot price.

Bonds & Escrow Safety

Both buyer and seller lock a small refundable Lightning deposit (fidelity bond) into escrow during the trade. When the fiat payment is confirmed, the escrow releases the Bitcoin to the buyer and refunds both bonds.
